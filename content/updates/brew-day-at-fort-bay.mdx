Brew days at Deep Dive start the way they do at breweries everywhere — with grain, water, and a plan. The difference is where it happens: a 26-by-26-foot, all-electric brewhouse tucked into Fort Bay Harbor on Saba.

Our brewhouse came from Alpha Brewing Operations in Nebraska and has been running since our first batch in December 2024. Brewing on a five-square-mile island means the schedule belongs to the ocean as much as to us — nearly every ingredient arrives on the shared container that reaches Saba roughly every two weeks, so each brew day is planned around the shipping calendar as carefully as the recipe.

A brew day runs the full circuit: milling malt, mashing in, lautering the wort clear, boiling with hops, then sending it through the heat exchanger into a waiting fermenter. By evening the tanks are doing the quiet work, and the brewhouse smells like the good part of the job.

If you are on the island and want to see it in person, come by for a tour — and you can find the finished product on tap and in cans around Saba and in Sint Maarten / Saint Martin.
